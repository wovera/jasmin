# Wovera fork of Jasmin

Wovera's maintained fork of Jasmin. We track our own mainline here and carry a small set of local patches; the
patch list, rationale, and validation live in our internal engineering docs, not in this repository.

- `origin` = this fork · `upstream` = `github.com/jookies/jasmin` (we never push to the upstream `master`).
- Mainline branch: **`master-wovera`**.
- Base: `789cfb5c` (txAMQP → pika port).

Local patches are kept small and, where they are general bug-fixes, offered upstream.
